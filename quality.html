Our Commitment

Quality Is Not Optional

At MidMach, quality is embedded in every step of our process—from initial engineering review through final inspection and shipment. Our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system ensures repeatability, traceability, and accountability.

We invest in our people, our processes, and our inspection technology to guarantee that every part leaving our facility meets or exceeds customer specifications.

How We Deliver

Quality Process Overview

A structured approach to manufacturing quality, from incoming material verification to final release.

🔍

Incoming Inspection

All raw materials are verified against mill certifications and purchase order requirements before entering production. Dimensional, chemical, and visual inspections ensure compliance from the start.

⚖

In-Process Monitoring

First-article inspections, statistical process control, and operator checkpoints at every critical operation. Real-time monitoring catches deviations before they become defects.

📈

Final Inspection

Every completed part undergoes dimensional verification on our CMM, visual inspection, and functional testing as required. Nothing ships without sign-off from our quality team.

📋

Testing & Validation

Non-destructive testing, hardness testing, surface finish verification, and pressure testing capabilities to validate performance-critical parts and assemblies.

📝

Documentation

Complete quality documentation packages including inspection reports, material certifications, certificates of conformance, and first-article inspection reports (FAI) per AS9102.

📦

Packaging & Traceability

Parts are carefully packaged and labeled with full lot traceability. Serialized tracking from raw material to finished product ensures complete supply chain visibility.

Philosophy

Continuous Improvement

Quality isn't a destination—it's a practice. We apply lean manufacturing principles and data-driven decision-making to improve every aspect of our operation.

  • Regular management reviews and quality objectives tracking
  • Root cause analysis and corrective action (CAPA) programs
  • Employee training and cross-functional skill development
  • Supplier quality management and approved vendor audits
  • Customer feedback integration and satisfaction monitoring
  • Annual internal and third-party surveillance audits
