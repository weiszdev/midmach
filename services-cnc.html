What We Do

Machining Capabilities

Our CNC department delivers repeatable, high-precision parts whether you need a single prototype or a production run of thousands.

⚙

Multi-Axis Turning

Live-tooling lathes with sub-spindles produce complex turned parts in a single setup, reducing cycle times and improving concentricity.

⚖

3-, 4- & 5-Axis Milling

Simultaneous multi-axis milling tackles complex 3D geometries, undercuts, and compound angles with minimal fixturing.

◊

Tight Tolerances

Routinely holding ±0.0005″ on critical dimensions with in-process gauging and climate-controlled inspection.

★

Complex Geometries

From thin-wall aerospace housings to intricate defense components, we program and machine parts others turn away.
