What We Do

Welding Capabilities

From single-piece prototypes to high-volume production runs, our robotic and manual welding capabilities cover the full spectrum of weldment needs.

⚙

MIG Welding (GMAW)

High-deposition robotic MIG welding for carbon steel, stainless steel, and aluminum assemblies with consistent penetration and minimal spatter.

⚖

TIG Welding (GTAW)

Precision TIG welding for thin-wall tubing, stainless steel, and applications requiring superior bead appearance and X-ray quality joints.

◊

Multi-Axis Articulation

6-axis robotic arms paired with 2-axis positioners reach complex joint geometries and weld in optimal positions for every seam.

★

High-Volume Production

Dual-station cells with automatic part loading keep the arc burning while operators load and unload, maximizing throughput and minimizing idle time.

Why Robotic

Benefits of Automated Welding

Robotic welding delivers measurable advantages over manual processes, especially on repeat production runs.

✓

Weld Consistency

Every weld is identical. Programmed parameters eliminate the variability inherent in manual welding for part-to-part repeatability.

⚡

Faster Cycle Times

Robotic cells weld 3–5x faster than manual processes with continuous arc-on time and automated part handling.

⚖

Reduced Costs

Lower per-piece costs through reduced labor, less rework, minimal consumable waste, and higher throughput on production volumes.

★

Superior Quality

Seam tracking, real-time voltage monitoring, and post-weld inspection ensure every joint meets or exceeds AWS D1.1 standards.
