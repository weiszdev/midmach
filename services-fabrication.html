Metal Fabrication

Full-service custom metal fabrication from raw material to finished, painted assemblies—all under one roof.

What We Do

Fabrication Capabilities

From a single bracket to complex multi-component weldments, our fabrication department handles every step of the process in-house.

⚙

Forming & Bending

CNC press brakes up to 500 tons with multi-axis back gauges produce precise bends in plate, sheet, and structural shapes to tight angular tolerances.

⚖

Punching & Shearing

CNC turret punch presses and hydraulic shears cut and perforate sheet metal at high speed with minimal setup time.

◊

Assembly & Fitting

Skilled fitters assemble complex structures using fixtures, jigs, and precision measurement to ensure dimensional accuracy before welding.

★

Finishing & Painting

In-house surface prep, primer, and topcoat painting including powder coat and wet spray for corrosion protection and aesthetic finish.

◆

Rolling & Plate Work

Plate rolls form cylinders, cones, and curved sections from heavy plate up to 1″ thick and 10′ wide for tanks, hoppers, and structural components.

♦

Custom Solutions

Our engineering team works with you from concept through production, providing DFM feedback to optimize your design for cost and manufacturability.
