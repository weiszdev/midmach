About MidMach

Over 60 years of precision manufacturing excellence, serving defense, aerospace, and industrial markets from Bismarck, North Dakota.

Our Story

Built on Decades of Excellence

MidMach traces its roots back to the early 1960s, when Midwestern Machine was founded in Bismarck, North Dakota. What started as a small machine shop serving the local agricultural and energy sectors quickly grew into a trusted name in precision manufacturing across the upper Midwest.

For decades, Midwestern Machine built a reputation for quality workmanship, reliable delivery, and the kind of hands-on customer service that only comes from a team that takes pride in every part they produce.

In 2014, Midwestern Machine became MidMach — a new name celebrating a fresh chapter while honoring the foundation of quality and innovation that defined the company from day one.

The rebrand marked the beginning of a major expansion. MidMach invested heavily in advanced manufacturing technology, including CLOOS robotic welding cells, LVD Strippit Phoenix fiber laser systems, and multi-axis CNC machining centers. The company moved into a purpose-built, 150,000+ square foot facility designed for high-volume production, precision work, and future growth.

Today, MidMach serves defense, aerospace, industrial, and energy clients nationwide with a full suite of manufacturing capabilities — all under one roof, all held to the highest quality standards.
