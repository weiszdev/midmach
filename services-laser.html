Our LVD Strippit Phoenix fiber laser systems combine raw cutting speed with automated load/unload towers, enabling lights-out production runs with minimal operator intervention.

Why Laser Cutting?

⚡

Speed

Fiber lasers process parts in seconds, not minutes. High rapid-traverse rates and pierce speeds keep throughput at peak levels.

◎

Accuracy

Consistent beam quality and servo-driven motion systems deliver ±0.002″ repeatability part after part, nest after nest.

✓

Edge Quality

Clean, oxide-free edges on most materials mean parts go straight to bending or welding with little to no secondary finishing.
